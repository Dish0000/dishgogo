# Privacy Policy **Last updated: 15 October 2026** ## 1. Introduction We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you contact us about bathroom renovations, home improvements or other services we provide through our website, social media pages or other communication channels. ## 2. Information We Collect When you contact us or request a quotation, we may collect the following information: - Your name and contact details, including your phone number and email address. - Your Auckland suburb or property address. - Photos and approximate measurements of your bathroom or property. - Your renovation requirements, preferred budget and product preferences. - Messages, enquiries and other information you voluntarily provide. - Other information reasonably necessary to assess your project and provide our services. You are not required to provide every type of information listed above. However, some information may be necessary for us to prepare an accurate quotation or arrange a site visit. ## 3. How We Use Your Information We may use your personal information to: - Respond to enquiries and messages. - Prepare quotations and discuss renovation options. - Arrange site inspections and appointments. - Provide bathroom renovation and home improvement services. - Communicate with you about your project, materials, scheduling and pricing. - Improve our customer service. - Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements. We will use your information for purposes reasonably connected with the reason it was collected, or as otherwise permitted by law. ## 4. Sharing Your Information We do not sell your personal information. Where necessary to provide our services, we may share relevant information with contractors, tradespeople, suppliers or service providers involved in your project. We may also disclose personal information where required or authorised by law. We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is handled appropriately when it is shared. ## 5. Cookies and Third-Party Services Our website or advertising platforms may use cookies, analytics tools or similar technologies to understand website activity, measure advertising performance or improve user experience. Third-party platforms, including social media and website hosting providers, may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Please refer to the relevant third-party provider's privacy policy for further details about its information practices. ## 6. Storage and Security We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, use, modification or disclosure. Personal information may be stored electronically through email, website hosting services or other business systems used to manage enquiries and projects. We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. ## 7. Access to and Correction of Your Information Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you may request access to personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate. If you wish to access, update or correct your personal information, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request in accordance with applicable privacy law. ## 8. External Links Our website or communications may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external websites you visit. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, business practices or legal requirements.
