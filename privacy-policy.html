Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Dish Meng & Co., operated by GT Link Limited (“we”, “us” or “our”), respects your privacy. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable New Zealand law, including the Privacy Act 2020.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us or request a quotation, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, Auckland suburb, project photos, measurements, service requirements, budget and messages. If you use our website forms, we also receive the information entered in those forms.

2. How we use information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, assess projects, prepare quotations, arrange site visits, provide and manage our services, communicate with you, and maintain appropriate business records.

3. When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with contractors, suppliers or service providers where reasonably necessary to assess or deliver your project, and where required or permitted by law. Our website and communications may use third-party services, such as website form, analytics, advertising or messaging providers, which may process information under their own privacy terms.

4. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, use, modification or disclosure. We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law.

5. Access and correction

You may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements. Please contact us using the details below.

6. Cookies and third-party services

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies through analytics, advertising, embedded chat or other third-party services. Those providers may collect technical or usage information according to their own policies and settings.
